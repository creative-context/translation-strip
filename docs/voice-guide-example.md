# My voice in English

An example voice guide for Translation Strip. Copy it, change it until it sounds like you, and pick your copy with Choose Voice Guide… in the app menu. It can be in any language; this one is in English. Everything in it is made up.

## How I sound

Friendly and clear. I get to the point in the first sentence, use contractions, and keep messages short. I sound like a colleague, not a form letter.

## Normal and Formal

- **Normal** is for my team and the people I work with every day. Casual, warm, plain words. "Hey", "Thanks!" and an emoji now and then are fine.
- **Formal** is for clients, other companies and people I don't know yet. Polite and complete sentences, still plain: no stiff phrases, no exclamation marks.

## What I never write

- "Per my last email", "circle back", "touch base", "going forward".
- "Warmest regards", "Cheers," as a sign-off.
- "Hope you're doing well" as an opener.
- More than one exclamation mark in a row, or in a formal message at all.
- Words in all caps for emphasis.

## How I ask for things

Plainly, with a please when it's a favor: "Could you please get me the signed form by the 12th? We need it to book the room." I don't apologize for asking.

## Examples

| I write | It should come out |
|---|---|
| ¿Me pasas el reporte cuando puedas? | Could you send me the report when you get a chance? |
| Perdón, no llego a la de las 3, ¿la movemos? | Sorry, I can't make the 3 o'clock. Can we move it? |
| Ya quedó, échale un ojo cuando puedas 👀 | It's done, take a look when you can 👀 |
| (Formal) Le confirmo que recibimos su pago, gracias. | I'm confirming we received your payment. Thank you. |
| (Formal) ¿Sería posible mover la entrega al lunes? | Would it be possible to move the delivery to Monday? |

## Names and terms that stay as they are

Product names, acronyms and people's names stay exactly as I write them, in their language. For example: "el dashboard de ventas" is "the sales dashboard", but "Q3", "OKR" and "Lucía" stay as they are.

## When something isn't clear

If a message could mean two different things and the difference matters, ask me one short question instead of guessing.
